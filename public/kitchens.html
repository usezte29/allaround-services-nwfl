How it goes

What to expect, start to finish.

No mystery, no disappearing act. The same person who quotes your kitchen is the person standing in it while it is being built.

Book the walkthrough

  1. We come and look

    Call or send the form and we will come out, measure, and talk through what you actually want changed.

  2. A free estimate, in writing

    You get a clear price with no obligation. Ask questions — that is what it is for.

  3. Selections

    Cabinetry, counters, tile, hardware, lighting. We will point you at good material and tell you where it is worth spending.

  4. Build

    Demolition, rough-in, cabinets, stone, tile, paint, trim. The owner is on site while it happens.

  5. Walk it together

    We walk the finished kitchen with you and fix anything that is not right before we call it done.
