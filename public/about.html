The short version

Steve is the one who shows up.

Most remodeling companies sell you the owner and send you a subcontractor. Here, the owner is present on every job site — which is why the reviews on this site keep using the same three words: honest, on time, fair.

It also means quality control is not a policy in a binder somewhere. It is a person standing in the room, looking at the work, while it is being done.

Twenty years on this coast has taught us what actually survives here. Salt air, hurricane season, summer sun and sandy ground are hard on a house, so we use only the best materials to make sure your remodel lasts for years — and we will tell you when the cheaper option is genuinely fine and when it is going to cost you twice.
